Evaluation meaning and response-timing limits

The existing protocol is chronological within-student, retrospective and teacher-forced: fixed weights predict each target using earlier indexed responses, including earlier validation/test responses. It is not student-disjoint, a global-calendar holdout or a certified real-time presentation-time evaluation. An earlier event index does not prove that its answer or correctness feedback had arrived.
